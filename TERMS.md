## Review

- Automated checks run on every commit in your pull request. If a formal check fails (format, authorship, size or build), we can't accept the pull request until you fix it.
- A code scanner and AI checks that we run on Anthropic's models review your app's code, texts and images. They show the moderator what to check and explain failed checks to you. They don't approve or reject apps.
- A moderator makes the decision: approves your app, asks for changes or rejects it.

## Removal and appeal

- If your app needs changes, we'll tell you. If you don't reply within 14 days, we remove it from the catalog.
- In case of a security vulnerability, a copyright issue or another serious problem, we may remove your app or one of its versions right away.
- We may reject or remove any app.
- When we reject your app, remove it or remove one of its versions, we tell you at the same time on GitHub, in your pull request or in an issue in this repository that mentions you: which term the decision is based on and why, whether automated checks were involved, and how to appeal.
- To appeal, reply to that message. A moderator reviews the decision and replies.
